# Logbook

A simple workout notebook. Pick your exercises, log sets like you would on paper, and it shows what you did last time and what to aim for today.

Progression is safe double progression: add reps until every set reaches the top of your rep range, then add the smallest weight step. Weight and reps never go up together. Two short sessions in a row bring the weight down about 10%, and it eases you back in after a break.

Everything is stored on your device (localStorage). Export a backup from Settings.
